# A. What We Collect

**Account info.** When you register, we store your Minecraft username and UUID, and — depending on how you sign up — your Discord ID and username, or an email address and a hashed password (we never store your actual password). Anything you add to your profile (social links, bio, avatar) is stored too.

**Activity while you play or chat.**
- Playtime, join/leave times, and which server you were on.
- Basic Discord activity (message count, voice time, reactions) is tracked for community stats, but we do not store the text of your Discord messages — only metadata like length and timestamp.
- When a message is relayed between Minecraft and Discord through our chat bridge, the actual text is stored briefly (a few hours) to make the bridge work, then automatically deleted.
- If our anti-spam/raid system flags your account, it may keep a short sample of the flagged message as evidence.

**Location.** We do not store your raw IP address. When you connect, your IP is hashed (one-way, can't be reversed) and used to look up a rough country/region/city. This is used to catch ban evasion, not to track you precisely.

**Moderation records.** Bans, mutes, kicks, and appeals you or others are involved in, including Discord ID and reason. If you report another player, we store your report, the reason, and any evidence you attach.

**Purchases.** If you buy something on the webstore, payment is handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. We keep a record of what was purchased, the amount, and your Minecraft username, plus Stripe's own reference IDs for that transaction.

**Support tickets, forum posts, poll votes, event RSVPs, badges, and vote-party history** — all tied to your account so we can respond to you or track community participation.

**Linked accounts.** If you connect a Discord, Twitch, or other platform account, we store the connection details needed to keep it working.

**Internal finance records.** We keep a separate financial ledger (accounts, vendors, invoices, budgets) for running the server. This is organizational bookkeeping used by finance staff, not general player data.

# B. How Long We Keep It

Most of what's listed above (moderation history, purchases, forum activity, reports) is kept long-term, since it's needed for ongoing moderation, appeals, and financial recordkeeping.

Some things are deleted automatically because they're short-lived by design:
- Chat bridge message text: within a few hours.
- Discord message/voice/reaction metadata: after about a year.
- Verification and password-reset codes: as soon as they expire or are used.

# C. Who Can See It

Your data is only accessible to Crafting For Christ staff who need it to do their job (admins, moderators, support, finance). It's never sold or handed to outside parties, except where required by Australian law. Some things — like vote counts, forum posts, and player stats — are public by nature, since they're visible on the server or website already.

Staff must keep anything not already public confidential, even after they stop being staff.

# D. Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. IP addresses are hashed before being stored. We hold no liability for a data breach or hack of our systems.

# E. Deletion Requests

Outside of the data that's automatically deleted (see Section B), we don't delete stored data on request. This is to preserve moderation history, prevent ban evasion, and meet our legal recordkeeping obligations. Data that's automatically purged (like chat bridge text) is gone for good once that happens — we can't retrieve it either.
